# Go-To-Market Plan

Digital Event Planning Assistant (Nigeria)

## 1. Overview

Nigeria’s event culture is massive, emotionally driven, and highly fragmented. This GTM plan focuses on launching a digital event-planning assistant that simplifies vendor discovery, planning workflows, and guest/gift management.

The strategy prioritizes:

- Fast validation
- Low-cost acquisition
- Community-driven growth
- Trust-building in a referral-heavy market

---

## 2. Target Audience

### Primary Users

- Brides & grooms
- Families planning burials
- Young adults planning birthdays, baby showers, graduations
- Event planners needing a digital workflow

### Secondary Users

- Vendors (venues, caterers, photographers, DJs, decorators)
- Asoebi sellers
- Gift registry partners

---

## 3. Positioning

**“Your smart event-planning assistant — from idea to celebration.”**

Differentiators:

- Smart questionnaire → instant plan
- Curated vendor recommendations
- Guest list + gift list tools
- Localized for Nigerian culture

---

## 4. Acquisition Channels

### Phase 1 — Zero-cost validation

- TikTok + Instagram reels showing the planning flow
- Twitter threads on “How to plan a Nigerian wedding under X budget”
- WhatsApp broadcast lists + community groups
- Partnerships with micro-influencers (event planners, makeup artists)

### Phase 2 — Growth

- Vendor referral program (vendors earn visibility)
- University ambassadors for birthdays & graduations
- SEO: “wedding venues in Lagos”, “burial planning checklist Nigeria”
- Paid ads targeting engaged couples

---

## 5. Activation Strategy

- Simple onboarding: “Answer 8 questions to get your event plan”
- Instant gratification: auto-generated vendor list + budget
- Save/share plan via WhatsApp
- Encourage users to invite co-planners (family, partner)

---

## 6. Retention Strategy

- Automated reminders (payments, asoebi deadlines)
- Vendor availability updates
- Gift list contributions
- Event countdown dashboard

---

## 7. Metrics to Track

- CAC (should be near zero early on)
- Vendor conversion rate
- Questionnaire completion rate
- Event plan shares
- Gift list contributions
- Vendor bookings facilitated

---

## 8. Launch Roadmap

### Month 1–2

- MVP launch
- 20–30 curated vendors
- Social media content engine

### Month 3–4

- Vendor onboarding portal
- Gift registry partnerships

### Month 5–6

- Expand to 3 major cities
- Introduce premium planning tools

---

## 9. Long-Term Vision

Become the digital backbone of Nigeria’s event industry — a trusted platform for planning, vendors, payments, and logistics.

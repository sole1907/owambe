# Business Model

Digital Event Planning Assistant (Nigeria)

## 1. Core Revenue Streams

### 1. Vendor Commissions (Primary)

- 5–10% commission on bookings made through the platform
- Vendors pay for visibility and guaranteed leads

### 2. Premium Planning Tools

Subscription tiers:

- Basic (free)
- Pro (₦3,000–₦5,000/month)
- Planner Pro (for event planners)

Features:

- Unlimited guests
- Advanced checklists
- Asoebi management
- Seating charts (future)

### 3. Gift Registry Commissions

- 3–5% commission on purchased gifts
- Cash gift processing fee

### 4. Vendor Ads & Boosted Listings

- Sponsored placements
- Featured vendor badges

### 5. Affiliate Partnerships

- Hotels
- Car rentals
- Decor suppliers
- Event insurance

---

## 2. Cost Structure

### Fixed Costs

- Cloud hosting (free tier initially)
- Domain + email
- Basic marketing

### Variable Costs

- Payment processing fees
- Vendor onboarding support
- Customer support

---

## 3. Scalability

The model scales with:

- More vendors
- More events
- More cities
- More premium features

Marketplace dynamics → exponential growth potential.

---

## 4. Competitive Advantages

- Localized for Nigerian culture
- Smart planning automation
- Trust-building through verified vendors
- Low-friction onboarding
- Multi-sided marketplace

---

## 5. Long-Term Expansion

- Pan-African expansion (Ghana, Kenya, South Africa)
- Corporate event planning tools
- AI-driven vendor pricing optimization
- Logistics & rentals marketplace
